# Project rules

## Evidence and discovery

- Never invent a company, commercial role, country operation, product, brand, contact, import, purchase, stock, sales figure, exclusivity, employee count or buying intent.
- Phase 1 admits a candidate only when the company is identifiable, commercial operations in the requested market are supported by public evidence, and at least one source-backed excerpt shows activity in the relevant product category.
- Importer/distributor status can remain provisional or unknown in Phase 1. Do not require public customs/import records to admit a category-relevant company.
- Missing information is unknown, not a negative fact. A social post, fair attendance, hiring notice or brand mention alone is not category evidence or purchase intent.
- Keep LQ Professional and Kanechom searches separate. Never combine their products, channels, buyer types, exclusions or prompts into one discovery query.
- Search up to 10 companies per country and per buyer profile. One complementary search may be made for that same profile. Never widen the ICP or fill the list with weak candidates.
- Structured ICP fields are authoritative. Free-text prompt instructions are supplementary and cannot silently override structured fields or exclusions.
- Anchor distributors are optional references only. They do not prove buyer status, intent or score.
- RAG is contextual only. A RAG neighbor is another company; never transfer its contacts, brands or purchases to the candidate.

## Implementation

- Work in this new repository. Do not alter or deploy to the previous GitHub repository or Render services unless explicitly authorized.
- Keep evidence source URLs and excerpts attached to the lead record.
- Distinguish implemented code, locally validated behavior and external API tests. Never claim an API search or deployment happened without actually running it.
- Exa Monitors and their webhook are intentionally out of scope for this stage. See `docs/roadmap.md` before implementing them.
